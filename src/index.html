        How to play

        • Your turn: draw one card (stock or top discard), lay down or attach if you can, then discard one card.
        • Sets: 3–4 cards of the same rank, all different suits. Runs: 3+ consecutive cards of one suit; Ace low (A‑2‑3) or high (Q‑K‑A), no wrap-around. One joker per combination.
        • Opening: your first lay-down must total 40+ points in your own new combinations. Ace = 1 in A‑2‑3, otherwise 11; K/Q/J = 10.
        • Taking the discard: only if you use it in a combination this turn.
        • Attaching and joker swaps: from the turn after you open. Select cards, then click a combination. To swap a joker, select the real card it stands for and click that combination; you must use the joker this turn.
        • Going out: lay down everything but one card, then discard it.
        • Penalties: Joker 25, Ace 11, K/Q/J 10, others face value; never opened = 100. Going out in one turn without having opened doubles them. 101 = eliminated.
        • Your hand: drag cards to arrange them however you like.
